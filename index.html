Vision:Gigtick is an artist-driven initiative that wants to culture local audiences across multiple genres in one night. While other management companies require a high fee, we offer three different genres for the price of one.

Mission:We believe great music and great venues deserve each other. Gigtick connects artists with breweries to create memorable live experiences, empowering performers and elevating spaces that celebrate community and creativity.
